## Overview

I provide cloud infrastructure consulting and implementation for international teams dealing with reliability, security, or operating costs that have outgrown their current platform. I turn those constraints into a practical architecture that your team can operate and evolve.

## What's Included

- **Cloud-native architecture design** - Microservices, event-driven systems, and serverless patterns
- **Container orchestration** - Kubernetes cluster design, deployment strategies, and operational runbooks
- **MicroVM infrastructure** - Firecracker-based isolation for multi-tenant workloads
- **CI/CD pipelines** - Automated build, test, and deployment workflows
- **Infrastructure as Code** - Terraform, Pulumi, or CloudFormation implementations
- **Performance optimization** - Right-sizing, autoscaling, and cost analysis

## Technologies

Kubernetes, Docker, Firecracker, Cloud Native Buildpacks, Terraform, AWS, GCP, Cloudflare Workers, and more.
