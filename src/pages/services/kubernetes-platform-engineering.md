## Build a reliable Kubernetes platform

I provide Kubernetes platform consulting for international engineering teams facing delivery bottlenecks or operational risk. I help design, operate, and improve a platform without adding unnecessary complexity, so developers can ship confidently and operators can understand what happens when something goes wrong.

## What I can help with

- **Platform architecture** — Cluster topology, namespaces, networking, storage, and workload isolation
- **Workload delivery** — Deployment conventions, Helm, GitOps workflows, rollbacks, and release safety
- **Reliability** — Health checks, resource requests, autoscaling, disruption budgets, and failure recovery
- **Observability** — Metrics, logs, tracing, alerting, and practical production runbooks
- **Security** — Least-privilege access, network policies, secrets handling, image hygiene, and hardening
- **Developer experience** — Documentation and paved paths that make the right deployment easy

## A practical engagement

We start with the current platform, constraints, and the incidents or delivery bottlenecks you want to solve. I then propose a focused architecture and implementation plan, validate it incrementally, and leave behind documentation your team can operate.

This is a good fit for teams migrating to Kubernetes, reducing platform toil, or preparing a growing production service for more demanding workloads.

## Technologies

Kubernetes, Docker, Cloud Native Buildpacks, Terraform, Linux, Prometheus, Grafana, containerd, and cloud or bare-metal infrastructure.
